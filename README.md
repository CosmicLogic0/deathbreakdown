# Death Breakdown

When you die, see exactly what happened. A clean recap shows every hit from your last 10 seconds:
who or what hit you, the damage type, the damage, how much your armor and enchantments blocked, and the final blow.

- Keybind to reopen your last recap
- Config for the time window and what's shown
- Small and modpack friendly
- Paper companion: the same recap in chat, or as a clickable book, for players without the mod

| Folder | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `mod/` | Forge + NeoForge mod |
| `paper/` | Paper plugin (uses LogicLib for Paper) |
| `ci/` | Automatic tests, copied from the LogicLib repos |
| `docs/` | CurseForge listing text |
| `media/` | Icons and screenshots |

Targets: Minecraft 1.12.2, 1.16.5, 1.18.2, 1.19.2, 1.20.1, 1.21.1, 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2, 26.3.
